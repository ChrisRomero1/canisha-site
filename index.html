Get in Touch
How far in advance should I book?

4–6 weeks for smaller events, 3–6 months for weddings or large corporate events. Popular dates fill fast — a deposit locks yours in immediately.

Do you provide bar equipment?

Yes — we bring everything for a full mobile bar setup including equipment, mixers, garnishes, and ice. You can also provide your own alcohol and we handle the rest.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Fully licensed and insured to serve alcohol at private and public events in Tennessee. Documentation available on request.

What areas do you serve?

Based in Chattanooga, TN — serving Hamilton County, Bradley County, and North Georgia. Travel fees may apply for events 30+ miles out.

How do deposits and payments work?

A deposit secures your date. Remaining balance due on or before the event. We accept credit cards, Zelle, and Cash App.

Can you do non-alcoholic events?

Yes — full mocktail and signature non-alcoholic beverage service available. Perfect for corporate events, family gatherings, or any dry event.
