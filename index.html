Common Questions

FAQ

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance for smaller events, and 3–6 months for weddings or large corporate events. Popular dates fill up fast — a deposit locks in your date immediately.

Do you provide the bar equipment?

Yes — we bring everything needed for a full mobile bar setup including equipment, mixers, garnishes, and ice. You can also opt to provide your own alcohol and we'll handle everything else.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed and insured to serve alcohol at private and public events in Tennessee. Documentation available upon request.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Chattanooga, TN and serve the surrounding areas including Hamilton County, Bradley County, and North Georgia. Travel fees may apply for events 30+ miles out.

How do deposits and payments work?

A deposit is required to secure your date. The remaining balance is due on or before the event date. We accept all major payment methods including credit cards, Zelle, and Cash App.

Can you accommodate non-alcoholic events?

Yes — we offer full mocktail and signature non-alcoholic beverage service. Perfect for corporate events, family gatherings, or any event where you want the experience without the alcohol.
